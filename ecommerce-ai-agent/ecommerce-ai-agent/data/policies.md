# TechZone Electronics - Store Policies

## Shipping Policy
- Standard shipping (within city): $5, 1-2 business days
- Express shipping (nationwide): $15, 3-5 business days
- FREE shipping on orders over $100
- Shipping hours: Monday to Saturday, 9 AM - 6 PM
- Tracking number sent via email within 24 hours of dispatch

## Return & Exchange Policy
- Returns accepted within 14 days of delivery
- Items must be in original condition with packaging and accessories
- Free returns for defective or damaged items
- Customer covers return shipping for change-of-mind returns
- Opened smartphones and laptops are non-returnable unless defective
- Refunds processed within 5-7 business days after inspection

## Warranty Policy
- All devices come with 1-year manufacturer warranty
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects only
- Warranty does NOT cover: water damage, drops, unauthorized repairs
- To claim warranty: contact support@techzone.com with order number
- Warranty repair turnaround: 7-14 business days
